Identity & Message

Group Background

🛡️

Bulletproof Origin

Originally named Bangtan Sonyeondan (Bulletproof Boy Scouts), the group set out to shield youth against prejudice, criticism, and social expectations aimed at them like bullets. In 2017, they introduced their extended English brand, Beyond The Scene.

✍️

Direct Artistry

Unlike standard commercial acts, BTS members co-write and self-produce their songs. They candidly address mental health, youth hardships, socio-economic divides, self-acceptance, and the philosophical search for purpose.

🕊️

Speak Yourself

Collaborating with UNICEF for the global #LoveMyself campaign, BTS spoke at the United Nations General Assembly, urging youth everywhere to celebrate their identity and speak their truth regardless of origin.

The Evolution

Milestones & Eras

2013 — 2014

Debut & School Trilogy

Debuted on June 13, 2013, with 2 Cool 4 Skool. Their raw hip-hop tracks like "No More Dream" and "N.O" confronted the immense educational pressure placed on teenagers.

2015 — 2016

The Most Beautiful Moment in Life & Wings

Achieved their first music show win with "I Need U". In 2016, Wings and "Blood Sweat & Tears" brought mainstream international recognition, entering the Billboard 200 at #26.

2017 — 2019

Love Yourself & Global Phenomenon

Won Top Social Artist at the BBMAs. Love Yourself: Tear became the first South Korean album to peak at #1 on the US Billboard 200, followed by selling out Wembley Stadium in London.

2020 — 2021

Billboard Hot 100 Dominance

Released "Dynamite", making history as the first all-South Korean act to hit #1 on the Billboard Hot 100. Followed with #1 smashes "Life Goes On", "Butter", and multiple Grammy nominations.

2022 & Beyond

Proof & Chapter 2

Released anthology record Proof marking 9 historic years. Members commenced chart-topping solo albums and completed their national service ahead of their global reunion.

Historic Chart Records

First South Korean act to top both the Billboard Hot 100 and Artist 100 simultaneously. Also the first group since The Beatles to earn three #1 albums on the Billboard 200 in less than a year.

Global Stadium Footprint

First Asian act in history to headline and sell out London's Wembley Stadium, the Rose Bowl in California, and Citi Field in New York, demonstrating unprecedented worldwide appeal.

Cultural Diplomatic Merits

Youngest recipients of South Korea's prestigious Order of Cultural Merit, and appointed Special Presidential Envoys for Future Generations and Culture.

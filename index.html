Origins

Group Background

🎙️

The Name & Meaning

Bangtan Sonyeondan translates to "Bulletproof Boy Scouts." The name signifies blocking out the stereotypes, criticisms, and unreasonable expectations aimed at young people like bullets. In 2017, they expanded the acronym to mean "Beyond The Scene", symbolizing youth moving forward into the future.

✍️

Artistic Philosophy

Formed by Big Hit Entertainment under producer Bang Si-hyuk, BTS distinguished themselves from the start by actively writing, composing, and producing their own music. Their discography tackles mental health, youth struggles, socio-economic pressures, and self-acceptance.

🌍

Global Message: Self-Love

BTS spearheaded the worldwide #LoveMyself campaign in collaboration with UNICEF. In 2018, RM delivered an address at the United Nations General Assembly, urging youth everywhere to "Speak Yourself," regardless of who you are or where you come from.

Milestones

Chronological History

2013

Debut & School Trilogy

Debuted on June 13, 2013, with single album 2 Cool 4 Skool and title track "No More Dream". Songs in this era critiqued intense educational pressure placed on youth in South Korea.

2015 – 2016

The Most Beautiful Moment in Life (HYYH) & Wings

BTS earned their first music show win with "I Need U". Later, their 2016 album Wings and title track "Blood Sweat & Tears" brought mainstream international recognition, entering the Billboard 200 at #26.

2017 – 2018

Love Yourself & Global Breakthrough

BTS won Top Social Artist at the Billboard Music Awards in 2017. Love Yourself: Tear (2018) became the first album by a Korean act to top the US Billboard 200, followed by a sold-out worldwide stadium tour.

2020 – 2021

Billboard Hot 100 #1s & English Hits

BTS released "Dynamite", making them the first all-South Korean act to reach #1 on the US Billboard Hot 100. They followed this with historic chart-toppers "Life Goes On", "Butter", and "Permission to Dance", earning multiple Grammy nominations.

2022 & Beyond

Anthology & Chapter 2

Released the anthology album Proof celebrating 9 years together. Members embarked on acclaimed solo albums, world tours, and completed mandatory military service, preparing for a grand reunion.

Billboard Dominance

First all-South Korean act to top both the Billboard Hot 100 and Artist 100 simultaneously. Also the first group since The Beatles to earn three #1 albums on the Billboard 200 in less than a year.

Global Stadium Tours

First Asian act to headline and sell out London's Wembley Stadium, the Rose Bowl in California, and Citi Field in New York, demonstrating unprecedented global ticket-selling power.

Cultural Merits

Youngest-ever recipients of South Korea's Order of Cultural Merit awarded by the President, acknowledging their contribution to spreading Korean culture and language across the globe.
